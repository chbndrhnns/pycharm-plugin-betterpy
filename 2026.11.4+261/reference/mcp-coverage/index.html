BetterPy MCP coverage checklist¶

A checked box requires meaningful direct success, a semantic failure with no unintended effects, all declared variants, three independent Luna sessions, and verified cleanup. Missing-file and empty-result cases remain supporting tests. They do not complete the behavioral checklist. Fingerprints must match the current fixture definitions; stale evidence is ignored. Retained failed attempts remain in the JSON history. Validation uses the newest observed artifact and requires every designated scenario to meet its own repeat count.
